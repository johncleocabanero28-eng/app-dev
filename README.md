# My Favorite Movie: 

## *About*

*How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days* is a 2003 American romantic comedy film directed by Donald Petrie. It follows two people who start dating for completely different reasons, not knowing that they're both playing their own little games.

Andie wants to prove that she can make a man leave her within 10 days, while Ben bets that he can make any woman fall in love with him in the same amount of time.

I love this movie because of its hilarious storyline, iconic moments, and the chemistry between the main characters. It's the kind of rom-com that makes you wish you could experience that early-2000s kind of love.

"Sometimes the best love stories start with the worst intentions."


## *Main Characters*

1. *Andie Anderson (Kate Hudson)* – A writer for a women's magazine who takes on a challenge to make a guy break up with her in just 10 days. She's independent, outspoken, and honestly, her personality makes the movie even better.
2. *Benjamin Barry (Matthew McConaughey)* – An advertising executive who bets that he can make a woman fall in love with him in 10 days. Confident, charming, and a little too sure of himself.
3. *Michelle Rubin (Kathryn Hahn)* – Andie's best friend and coworker who gives her advice throughout the whole experiment. She's hilarious and adds to the chaos.
